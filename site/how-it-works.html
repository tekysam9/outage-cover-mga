How it works

Parametric cover pays on a measured event, not on proof of loss.

1

Quote

Limit is 50% of your cold-chain inventory, up to $50,000. Price depends on your area’s outage risk, your trigger choice and whether you have a sensor.

2

Waiting period

Cover starts 24 hours after you buy. Outage time before that does not count.

3

Trigger

When covered outage time reaches your trigger, the payout is due. Tiered cover pays 25% at 4 hours, 50% at 8 and 100% at 24.

4

Payment

First payment is held until your bank account is verified. Payouts above $15,000 need a second person’s approval.

What the rules look like

QuestionAnswer
Who decides a payout?Fixed rules, version trigger-rules-v1. Never AI.
What if data sources disagree?At least two sources must agree within 30 minutes of duration, else a person reviews it at the conservative reading.
Can the same outage pay twice?No. Each event is recorded once per policy.
Do my rates change mid-term?No. Your policy keeps the rate and rule versions it was sold on.

Honest limits

  • Basis risk: you could have a bad outage that the data does not show, and then no payout is due. That is the trade for speed.
  • Covers power outages affecting your location, not equipment failure or other perils.
  • Texas first. Florida and New York come later. California is closed.
