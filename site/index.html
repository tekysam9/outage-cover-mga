Traditional spoilage claim

  • Prove what spoiled, item by item
  • Wait for an adjuster

Outage Cover parametric

  • Pays on a measured outage, not on proof of loss

Questions

Is Outage Cover an insurance company?

No. We’re building a managing general agent. Policies are meant to be issued by a licensed carrier, and we’re not selling anywhere until the licensing and carrier arrangements are in place. This site shows the product as it will work.

Where will it be available?

Texas first. Florida and New York come later, subject to counsel review. California is closed for now.

What counts as an outage?

An outage at your ZIP confirmed by independent data sources that agree on duration, after your 24-hour waiting period. If sources disagree, a person reviews it.

How fast is payment?

We target payment within 72 hours of a confirmed outage. Your first payout is held until your bank account is verified, and payouts above $15,000 need a second approval.

Can I sell this to my customers or members?

Yes. Agents, associations and equipment or POS partners can quote and bind through the portal or API. See Partners.
